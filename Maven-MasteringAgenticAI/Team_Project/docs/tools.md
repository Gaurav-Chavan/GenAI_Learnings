# Read-only tools

Generated by `claim_triage.tools.toolbox`. Every tool is read-only, validates its arguments, limits its results, answers as of a date, and never returns personal data or labels.

| Tool | What it answers | Arguments |
|---|---|---|
| `get_claim` | Details of one claim: amounts, dates, provider, the documents on file (types only), and the scrubbed description. | `claim_id` (required) |
| `get_claim_documents` | The text of the documents on file for one claim (bill, discharge summary, death certificate and so on), as scrubbed text. Read these when a finding might be explained, or contradicted, by what the documents say. | `claim_id` (required) |
| `get_claim_facts` | The prepared fact sheet for a claim to assess: policy age, prior claims, bill vs peers, duplicates, area risk, documents on file, and other signals, all computed as of the filing date. Empty fields mean 'not applicable'. | `claim_id` (required) |
| `get_area_signal` | Whether the claim comes from an area with a high rate of reviewer-confirmed fraud, with a plain-words reason. | `claim_id` (required) |
| `get_customer_summary` | Profile and history summary of a customer (coarse bands only, never identity). | `customer_id` (required) |
| `get_policy` | Details of one policy: cover, dates, status, nominee relation and declared conditions. | `policy_id` (required) |
| `get_person_timeline` | A customer's events (policy changes, nominee/phone/address/bank changes, claims), newest first. | `customer_id` (required), `as_of`, `limit` |
| `find_similar_claims` | Earlier claims that look like this one: same invoice, near-duplicate treatment, same vehicle and incident date, or paid to the same account for another customer. Each match lists why. | `claim_id` (required), `limit` |
| `get_provider_stats` | A hospital or garage: registration status, recent volume, and typical bill for a procedure compared with peers. | `provider_id` (required), `procedure_code`, `as_of` |
| `get_vehicle_inspections` | Inspections and repairs recorded for a vehicle (by its token), newest first. | `vehicle_token` (required), `as_of`, `limit` |
| `find_shared_entities` | Other customers who share this customer's phone, address or bank-account token, and other customers whose claims were paid to the same account recently. Household members (same address) are marked. | `customer_id` (required), `as_of`, `window_days` |
| `search_documents` | Keyword (BM25) search over claim documents or claim descriptions. | `query` (required), `source`, `as_of`, `limit` |
| `search_knowledge` | Search our knowledge for a claim: fraud-pattern cards, policy clauses, and earlier claims whose outcome a reviewer confirmed (precedents), found by keyword and by links in the graph, best first. Everything returned is context to weigh, never proof. Only decisions made before this claim was filed are used. | `claim_id` (required), `query` (required), `limit` |
